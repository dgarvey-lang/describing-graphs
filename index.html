    Key ideas
    Real zero (x-intercept)
    An x-value where f(x) = 0. The graph crosses or touches the x-axis there. A factor with an odd exponent crosses the axis. A factor with an even exponent touches it and bounces back.
    Turning point
    A point where the graph changes from rising to falling, or from falling to rising.
    Relative maximum
    A turning point that is the top of a “hill.” It is higher than the points near it, but it does not have to be the highest point on the whole graph.
    Relative minimum
    A turning point that is the bottom of a “valley.” It is lower than the points near it.
    How many?
    A polynomial of degree n has at most n real zeros and at most n − 1 turning points.
    Writing answers
    Write a relative max or min as a point (x, y). The relative maximum value is the y-coordinate.
    On a TI-84 graphing calculator
    1. Press Y= and type the function. Press GRAPH.
    2. Press 2nd TRACE to open CALC.
    3. Choose 2: zero, 3: minimum, or 4: maximum.
    4. Left Bound? Move the cursor to the left of the point and press ENTER.
    5. Right Bound? Move to the right of the point and press ENTER.
    6. Guess? Move near the point and press ENTER.
    7. Round the X and Y values to the nearest hundredth.

    The Calculator buttons under the graph work the same way.
